# Week 5: Biodiversity Metrics

## Core Questions

- What do richness, turnover, endemism, and rarity measure?
- How do metric choices change ecological interpretation?

## Topics

- Alpha, beta, and gamma diversity.
- Species richness and sample-size effects.
- Range size, weighted endemism, corrected weighted endemism, and rarity.
- Spatial aggregation and uncertainty in biodiversity surfaces.

## Applied Lab

Compute gridded richness and endemism metrics from occurrence data and compare results across grid resolutions.

## Deliverable

Submit two biodiversity maps at different grains with a short interpretation of scale sensitivity.
