# Week 8: Phylogenetic and Functional Diversity

## Core Questions

- How do evolutionary history and traits change biodiversity priorities?
- What assumptions enter when combining spatial, trait, and phylogenetic data?

## Topics

- Phylogenetic diversity and evolutionary distinctiveness.
- Functional diversity, trait spaces, and missing trait data.
- Community phylogenetic and functional structure.
- Spatial mismatch among trees, traits, distributions, and occurrence records.

## Applied Lab

Join species-by-site data with a trait table or phylogeny and calculate one phylogenetic or functional diversity metric.

## Deliverable

Submit a short analysis showing how taxonomic and phylogenetic or functional diversity differ across space.
