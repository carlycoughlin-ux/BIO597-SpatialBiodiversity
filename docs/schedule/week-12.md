# Week 12: Ordination, Gradients, and Evolutionary Diversity

## Core Questions

- How do geographic space and environmental space differ?
- Does community composition track environmental gradients?
- How do phylogenetic and functional diversity change biodiversity interpretation?

## Concepts

- PCA, PCoA, and NMDS.
- Environmental gradients.
- Community composition.
- Environmental filtering.
- Spatial structure.
- Phylogenetic diversity and evolutionary distinctiveness.
- Functional diversity, trait spaces, and missing trait data.

## Python Tools

- scikit-learn.
- scipy.
- matplotlib.

## Applied Lab

Students perform ordination on environmental variables or community dissimilarities, compare geographic and environmental space, and optionally add trait or phylogenetic information to evaluate functional or evolutionary diversity.

## Deliverable

Submit an ordination figure and a short analysis explaining whether community composition tracks environmental space, geographic space, or both.
